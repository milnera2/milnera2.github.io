Selected Projects

This document outlines several completed and ongoing technical and creative projects.

Modified “Turing Test” with an English Focus

The goal of this project is to both inform about and assess Generative AI’s ability to participate in creative writing and compare its output to that created by humans. The assessment is conducted via a modified “Turing Test” to see if readers are able to differentiate between human and AI writers.

Chemical “Sniffing” Robot Dog Attachment

Norse IoT is partnering with Duke Energy to develop an advanced air quality monitoring device designed to mount onto a robot dog. The device will detect and measure concentrations of various airborne chemicals, with a primary focus on monitoring hydrogen levels to identify potential leaks from large-scale battery systems and on-site facilities.

WebSocket Workshop

An interactive, hands-on activity to teach participants about WebSocket technology and wiring simple circuits. It is designed to allow students of all ages to get interactive experience through the workshop. Topics such as IoT and various hardware are covered in the presentation.

Search and Rescue Coordinator (SARCO)

A prototype device designed to enhance communication and coordination between on-field teams and mission control in urban search and rescue (SAR) operations. The device was named SARCO (Search and Rescue Coordinator), and integrates a GPS module with a Raspberry Pi to provide precise location data. The electronics are contained in a 3D-printed case attached to a common hiking stick.

Braille Education Device (B.E.D.)

A prototype educational device for Braille (B.E.D.) that features a 3D-printed casing and servo motors controlled by a Raspberry Pi Pico. The design allows the servos to rise and fall, forming the corresponding Braille characters. The primary goal was to create a cost-effective and easy-to-implement solution for Braille education.

ASE230 - Server Side 

Deployment of Laravel, MYSQL, Nginx, Docker, and other technologies to build complete, functioning apis. Requires practice with multiple industry level tech stacks focused on production ready work.
